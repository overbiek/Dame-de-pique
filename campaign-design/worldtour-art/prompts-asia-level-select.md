# Asia: the 10 level-select pictures (one image per city)

**Make 10 separate images, one for each of the 10 cities. One image per prompt, one prompt at a time.** Do not ask Gemini for a collage, a grid, a contact sheet or several cities in one picture. Every prompt below produces exactly **one single full-frame picture of one city**.

Each picture is the full-screen postcard that city's ten tables sit on, the same job `Cities/rome.png` does for Rome and the same as the Europe level-select pictures (`prompts-europe-level-select.md`). They are NOT the location stills.

## How to make each image

1. Start a **new chat** in the Gemini app for each city (so the cities do not mix).
2. **Attach `afbeeldingen/World Tour/Cities/rome.png`** as the style reference, every time.
3. Paste **one** complete prompt below (each one already contains the style, the city and the layout, so you only paste one block).
4. Generate **one** image, 16:9 wide. If it is not right, regenerate that same city; do not move on to the next city until this one is good.
5. Save it at full size (do not crop) as `afbeeldingen/World Tour/Cities/<id>.png` using the id in the heading, and tell Claude. Claude converts it, adds it to the app and makes Enter City work for that city (a city without a picture uses the side panel).

What every picture must have (already written into each prompt): a glamorous golden-hour or dusk look, a luxurious terrace in the lower foreground with a round card table (cards fanned on it, a small brass lamp, flowers, one drink, cushioned chairs, lanterns), the whole city recognisable behind and below it, small anonymous guests only (never a clear face), no readable text anywhere, a calm darker top strip for the title and a calm bottom strip for the row of table buttons.

The drinks suit each place (Riyadh has no alcohol, so coffee and dates).
