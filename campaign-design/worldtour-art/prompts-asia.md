- **Part 1: location stills (30).** Three per city, the same job and the same look as `stills-prologue-and-europe.md` section C: `_1` = Location A (tables 1-4 of the city), `_2` = Location B (tables 5-9), `_k` = the Keeper's Table venue (table 10). - **Part 2: level-select pictures (10).** The full-screen postcard each city's ten tables sit on, the same job and look as `prompts-europe-level-select.md` (and `Cities/rome.png`).

# PART 1: LOCATION STILLS (30)

## Rules for every still (same as Europe)

- **Realistic, photographic.** Real places, real light, real lenses. Not illustration, not painterly, not CGI.
- **16:9 landscape**, 1920x1080 or larger.
- **No readable text, no logos, no brand names, no flags with text.** That includes shop signs, neon signs and billboards: show the glow and shapes, not letters. The game puts its own text on top.
- **No recognisable faces.** Public locations (`_1`, `_2`) may show small anonymous figures far away, from behind or in motion blur. Keeper's Table venues (`_k`) are always **empty of people**.
- **Leave the lower third calm** (a table, pavement, floor), because dialogue cards sit there.
- **Dark, warm and a bit moody**, to sit with the game's emerald and brass. The script has day scenes (Riyadh souq, Mumbai harbour): keep them in low golden light with deep shadow, not bright postcards.
- Four tournament tables stand in each public location (five in Location B), so the public stills show **a few official round card tables with dark green baize** set up in the scene, small and tidy, never the main subject.
- Save as `afbeeldingen/World Tour/Cities/<id>.jpg` (full size, do not crop) and tell Claude.

Every prompt ends with: `Photorealistic, natural film grain, shot on a full-frame camera, no text, no logos, no watermark.`
