# World Tour country champions: one ChatGPT portrait each (Asia, 10)

No reference image, one portrait per champion. Save as `afbeeldingen/World Tour/Asia/champions/<country>.jpg` (full size). Ids in the game: `wt_champion_<cc>` (sau ind tha vnm phl idn twn chn kor jpn).

Shared framing in every prompt: plain flat mid-grey studio backdrop, square, head and shoulders, eyes about 40% from the top, head and both shoulders well inside the central 75% with space around them, soft warm key light from the front-left, photorealistic. If ChatGPT adds a border or vignette: "same picture, but a completely flat grey background and no border or vignette".

**The champions come from all parts of society, on purpose** (owner, 2026-11-03): a night-market noodle seller, a motorbike-taxi driver, a jeepney driver, a student, a retired factory worker, a convenience-store part-timer, a community organiser, a sushi chef, a trainee teacher, and one formal professional (Riyadh, where the script itself is about etiquette). Clothes are ordinary and worn, not costume and not luxury; the face and the one signature detail do the work. Keep the people dignified: no poverty clichés, no props.

Where the script gives a clue (age group, temperament, how they play) the prompt follows it. **Gender, age and job are my choice where the script says "they" (India, Thailand, Indonesia, Taiwan) or says nothing (Vietnam, Japan, Korea)**: five men and five women, change any of them freely. The Philippines champion is **Marco**, the Manila crew leader who becomes Jester's friend (the script says "he"). Each prompt has a "signature detail": one bold, natural feature that survives the seal window and the ~35 px avatar and makes the person recognisable.

The portraits will sit inside each country's gold seal, like Europe's. **The Asian gold seals do not exist yet**: run `make_champion.py` once they do (add the ten files to `SEAL_FILE`, the ten raw pictures to `RAW`).
