Credits

World Tour map

The Europe and world maps, and the rotating globe on the Campaign screen, were built from the following open data. Thank you to everyone who makes it available.

  • Map imagery: NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble: Next Generation (shaded relief and bathymetry, produced by Reto Stöckli at NASA Goddard), delivered through NASA's Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS).
    We acknowledge the use of imagery provided by services from NASA's Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS), part of NASA's Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS).
  • Elevation (the relief of the land): Terrain Tiles on AWS (Tilezen, formerly Mapzen), which combine several public elevation datasets, among them ETOPO1 (DOC/NOAA/NESDIS/NCEI), GMTED2010 and SRTM (courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey), and EU-DEM (Copernicus data, European Environment Agency), plus national datasets such as those of the UK Environment Agency, Kartverket (Norway) and Geoscience Australia. The full list of sources and licences is at github.com/tilezen/joerd.
  • Country outlines: Natural Earth (naturalearthdata.com), public domain.

The maps are rendered and drawn for the game from these sources; any mistakes in them are ours.
