Delete it yourself in the game

  1. Open Dame de Pique and log in.
  2. On the main menu, tap ⚙ Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account….
  4. Enter your password and tap Delete forever.

Your account is erased immediately and you are logged out on every device.

Can't log in? Ask us to delete it

Email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the recovery email saved on the account (or tell us the username), with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it and confirm by reply, normally within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your username, hashed password, nickname, avatar and recovery email
  • Your statistics, ranked rating, daily challenge scores and achievements
  • Your campaign progress, in-game credits, unlocked and purchased items and equipped cosmetics
  • Your friends list and friend requests
  • All login sessions

Nothing is kept for later: there is no recovery after deleting. A game you are playing at that moment finishes without counting toward any record. See the privacy policy for the rest.
