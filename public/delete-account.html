Delete your Dame de Pique account

In the game

  1. Log in and open ⚙ Settings from the main menu.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account….
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete forever.

Your account and data are erased immediately.

Can't log in?

Email damedepique@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and your username, and we will delete it.

What is deleted

Everything tied to your account: username, password, nickname, avatar, recovery email, statistics, scores, achievements, campaign progress, credits, unlocked items, friends and login sessions. Nothing is kept.
