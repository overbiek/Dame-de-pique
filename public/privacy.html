What we collect

You can play as a guest without an account. If you create an account, we store:

DataWhy
Username and a hashed password (the password itself is never stored)To let you log in
Nickname and avatarTo show you to other players at the table
Recovery email (optional)Only to send you a password reset link
Game data: statistics, ranked rating, daily challenge scores, achievements, campaign progress, in-game credits, items you unlocked or bought with credits, equipped cosmeticsTo run the game, the leaderboards and your progress
Friends and friend requestsTo let you play with people you choose
Login sessions (a random token per device)To keep you logged in

The game has no real-money purchases, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. Credits are earned by playing.

What stays on your device

The game stores a login token and your preferences (table theme, sound and music settings and volume, tutorial progress) in your browser's local storage so they survive a reload. You can clear them in your browser or app settings.

Who else handles your data

  • Railway hosts the game server and its database.
  • Resend sends the password reset email, if you saved a recovery email and ask for a reset.
  • Google Fonts: the game loads its typefaces from Google, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads. [OPTIONAL: remove this line if you later host the fonts yourself.]

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Other players

Your nickname, avatar, title, rank and in-game scores are visible to the other players you play with and on leaderboards. Your username, password and email are never shown to other players.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Password reset links expire after one hour. When you delete your account everything listed above is erased from the live database straight away (see how to delete your data). Your rights

You can see, correct or delete your data. Change your nickname, avatar and recovery email inside the game, and delete the account under Settings → Delete account. You can also write to us to ask for a copy of your data or to have it erased. If you are in the European Union you also have the right to object, to restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to your national data protection authority (in Belgium: the Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has made an account, tell us and we will delete it.

Security

Passwords are stored hashed, connections use HTTPS, and sessions end when you log out or reset your password. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Changes

If we change this policy the date at the top changes, and for significant changes we will tell players in the game.
