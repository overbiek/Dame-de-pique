Privacy Policy

Dame de Pique is operated by Robin Overbeeke, Belgium. Questions about your data: damedepique@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

You can play as a guest without giving us anything. If you create an account we store:

DataWhy
Username and a hashed passwordTo log you in
Nickname and avatarTo show you to other players and on leaderboards
Recovery email (optional)To send a password reset link
Game progress: statistics, rating, scores, achievements, campaign progress, in-game credits and unlocked itemsTo run the game
Friends and friend requestsTo let you play with people you choose
Login sessionsTo keep you logged in

Your login and settings are also saved in your browser or app on your device. The game has no ads, no analytics and no real-money purchases.

Who we share it with

  • Railway hosts the game and its database.
  • Resend sends the password reset email.
  • Google Fonts serves the game's fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Deleting it erases all of the data above from our database (how to delete).

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data, and you can complain to your national data protection authority. Write to damedepique@gmail.com.
